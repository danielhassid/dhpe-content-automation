# Channel Strategy — Learn with Spark

## Channel Identity

**Channel Name:** Learn with Spark
**Tagline:** "Something Amazing to Learn Every Day"
**Character:** Spark — a friendly animated child with blue hair and a star-shaped clip
**Language:** English only
**Target Age:** 2–8 years old (primary: 4–5)
**Platform:** YouTube only — no website, no social, just YouTube

## Content Pillars (rotating A → B → C)

| Type | Label | Description | Age Sweet Spot |
|------|-------|-------------|----------------|
| A | ABC & Numbers | Letters, counting, shapes, colors | 2–5 |
| B | Science for Kids | Why questions, nature, animals, space | 5–8 |
| C | Educational Stories | Animated moral stories with Spark | 3–7 |

## Differentiation from Competitors

| Competitor | Their Weakness | Our Advantage |
|-----------|----------------|---------------|
| CoComelon | 45-min compilations, thin educational value | 3-5 min structured lessons with clear learning goals |
| Little Baby Bum | Pure song loops | Active participation: "your turn!" moments |
| Blippi | Live action, adult energy, chaotic | Clean animation, calm consistent character |
| Moonbug Entertainment | High budget required | Niche depth + daily consistency beats budget |

**Our gap:** No top channel delivers 3–5 minute structured lessons with an explicit educational framework for the 2–5 age range. Most content is either too long or too vague educationally.

## Channel Positioning Statement

"Learn with Spark is the go-to channel for parents who want their 2–8 year olds to actually learn something, not just be distracted. Every 4-minute video delivers one clear educational outcome, structured like a mini-lesson: hook, lesson, practice, recap."

## Playlist Structure

Every video must be added to:
1. Its content-type playlist (ABC & Numbers / Science for Kids / Stories with Spark)
2. The "New Videos" playlist

Playlist IDs are stored in `credentials-guide.md`.

## Publishing Schedule

- **Time:** 14:00 UTC daily (US East Coast after-school window: 10am ET)
- **Frequency:** 1 video per day, 7 days a week
- **Consistency rule:** Never miss a day in the first 90 days — the algorithm rewards streaks

## Monetization Path

- **Month 1–3:** Build 90 videos, establish SEO presence
- **Month 3–6:** Aim for 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours
- **Month 6+:** YouTube Partner Program → AdSense revenue
- **Secondary:** Merchandise and sponsorships after 10,000 subscribers
