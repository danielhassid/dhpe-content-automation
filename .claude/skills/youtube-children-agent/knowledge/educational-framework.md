# Educational Framework — Hook → Lesson → Practice → Recap

Every single video follows this exact 4-section structure. No exceptions.

## Section 1: HOOK (0:00–0:20 | ~60 words)

**Purpose:** Create curiosity. Make the child lean forward.

**Rules:**
- Spark appears immediately and greets: "Hi friends, it's Spark! Ready to learn something amazing today?"
- Ask a question the child knows they don't yet know the answer to
- Name the topic explicitly within the first 5 seconds
- Use the words "today" and the primary keyword
- Never start with a slow intro — deliver on the title's promise within 10 seconds

**Examples:**
- ABC: "Do you know what letter makes the sound 'buh'? Spark knows! Let's find out together!"
- Science: "Why does the sky look blue? The answer is SO cool — let's discover it right now!"
- Story: "Spark found a mysterious glowing box. What do YOU think is inside?"

## Section 2: LESSON (0:20–2:30 | ~400 words)

**Purpose:** Deliver the educational content in digestible chunks.

**Structure:** 3–5 micro-chunks. Each chunk follows this pattern:
1. Introduce the concept
2. Show an example (visual + narration)
3. Repeat the key word or concept

**Rules:**
- Maximum 3 NEW concepts per video (cognitive load for age 2–5 = 2–3 items in working memory)
- Every new word is immediately defined in the next sentence
- Never exceed 8 words per sentence
- No abstract concepts unless grounded in a physical, visible example
- Repeat the primary keyword at least 5 times throughout the lesson
- Vocabulary ceiling: grade K–2 only

**Example chunk structure (Science — Why is the sky blue?):**
- "Light from the sun travels to Earth. The light has many colors. When it hits the air, blue light bounces the most. That bouncing fills the whole sky with blue!"

## Section 3: PRACTICE (2:30–3:30 | ~150 words)

**Purpose:** The child DOES something. Active participation, not passive watching.

**Rules:**
- Exactly 3 "your turn!" moments — no more, no fewer
- After each "your turn!", write [PAUSE 3s] in the script — Spark is silent, child answers
- Then Spark gives the correct answer as positive reinforcement
- Types of practice: repeat aloud, count with fingers, identify a color, answer a question
- Phrase: "Say it with me!", "Can you count that?", "What color is that?"

**Example:**
- "Now it's YOUR TURN! Can you say the letter B? [PAUSE 3s] That's right — B! You got it, friend!"

## Section 4: RECAP (3:30–4:30 | ~100 words)

**Purpose:** Reinforce the 3 things learned. Close the lesson loop.

**Structure:**
- "Today we learned..." followed by exactly 3 bullet-point recaps
- Each recap is one short sentence restating the core concept
- Subscribe hook: "If you learned something new today, click the subscribe button — there's more amazing stuff coming tomorrow!"
- End card: Spark waves goodbye. 10 seconds of static end card visual, no narration needed.

**Example:**
- "Today we learned: ONE — the letter B makes the 'buh' sound. TWO — B is for bear, ball, and banana. THREE — B is a curved letter with two bumps. Great job today, friends! Keep learning, keep growing, keep sparkling!"

## Iron Rules for All Scripts

- Max 8 words per sentence
- No sarcasm, no irony, no rhetorical questions
- No idioms (children take them literally)
- Primary keyword in first 20 words of the Hook
- Exactly 3 "[PAUSE 3s]" markers in the Practice section
- Total word count: 700–850 words
- End with subscribe hook before end card
