## מבנה: Hook → Explore → Wow-Fact → Recap

```
=== HOOK (0:00-0:20) ===
Hello friends! I'm Pip the owl.
Do you love ocean animals for kids?
Let's dive under the sea today!
What lives down in the blue water?
Come and find out with me.
[PAUSE 2s]

=== EXPLORE (0:20-2:30) ===
Look! A happy dolphin swims by.
The dolphin jumps high and splashes.
Dolphins love to play all day.
Can you wave to the dolphin?

Now look down at the rocks.
An octopus has eight wiggly arms.
The octopus can change its color!
One, two, three... let's count its arms.

Here comes a slow sea turtle.
The sea turtle has a hard shell.
It glides through the warm water.
Bye-bye, gentle sea turtle, swim along!

Look, a friendly shark says hello.
The shark has a big, soft smile.
This shark just loves to swim.
Sharks are ocean animals for kids too.

Down here, a tiny sea horse!
The sea horse holds on with its tail.
It is so small and cute.
Can you see the little sea horse?

And now... a giant blue whale!
The whale is the biggest ocean animal.
It sings a soft, happy song.
The whale waves its huge tail. Hello!
[PAUSE 2s]

=== WOW-FACT (2:30-3:30) ===
Here is something amazing, friends!
The blue whale is very, very big.
It is bigger than a school bus!
Its heart is as big as you.
Can you believe that? Wow!
Tell your mom and dad later!
[PAUSE 2s]

=== RECAP (3:30-4:30) ===
We met so many ocean animals today!
A dolphin, an octopus, a sea turtle.
A friendly shark, a sea horse, a whale.
Ocean animals for kids are so fun!
You learned them all so well.
I'm so proud of you, friend.
See you next time! Bye-bye! 🦉
```
